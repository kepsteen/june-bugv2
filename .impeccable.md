# Impeccable

Design context for **JuneBug**, consumed by the `impeccable` design skill and any
design work in this repo. Keep this file current as the product's look & feel evolves.

## Design Context

### Users

Developers who keep a daily journal. JuneBug gives them **one entry per calendar
day**, a rich text/markdown editor, and AI-assisted features (automatic titles,
long-lived "memories" extracted from entries, and personalized writing prompts
grounded in those memories).

The job to be done is **reflection, not productivity tracking**: sit down, capture
what happened and what you're thinking, and occasionally get a gentle nudge or
prompt. Sessions often happen at the end of the day or at night, so the interface
should feel comfortable in low light and never demanding.

### Brand Personality

- **Three words:** warm, gentle, human.
- **Emotional goal:** the interface should make the user feel **calm and safe** — a
  quiet, low-pressure space to think. No urgency, no gamification pressure, no
  dashboards-shouting-at-you energy.
- There is a mascot — the "june bug" — and small, earned moments of delight (the
  floating logo button, the happy-wiggle). Personality shows up in *restraint and
  small touches*, not loudness.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Keep and refine the current warm, organic aesthetic.** Do not redesign the brand.
- **Palette:** warm sand/cream neutrals in light mode, warm dark-brown in dark mode,
  with an olive/sage **green primary** and amber-leaning accents. All colors are
  authored in **OKLCH** (see `apps/web/src/index.css`). Neutrals are intentionally
  tinted toward the warm brand hue (~80) — preserve that warmth; never introduce cold
  grays, pure `#000`/`#fff`, or the cyan-on-dark / purple-gradient "AI" palette.
- **Type:** Nunito (sans / UI + body), PT Serif (serif, for editorial/journal voice),
  JetBrains Mono (code & metadata). Favor the serif for entry-reading moments to
  reinforce the "journal" feeling; keep UI chrome in Nunito.
- **Shape & texture:** soft rounded corners (`--radius: 0.625rem`), gentle borders,
  the notched editor panel. Tactile and cozy, like a worn notebook — not glassy,
  not neon, not flat-corporate.
- **Themes:** support **both** light and dark; dark must feel genuinely warm (brown,
  not slate) because so much journaling happens at night.
- **Anti-references:** generic SaaS dashboards, glassmorphism, glowing dark-mode
  accents, gradient text, colored side-stripe borders, identical card grids,
  hero-metric layouts.

### Design Principles

1. **Calm over clever.** Every screen should lower the user's heart rate. When in
   doubt, remove, quiet down, and add whitespace rather than add affordances.
2. **Warm, never cold.** Tint neutrals toward the brand hue; keep contrast readable
   but soft. Both themes should feel like paper and lamplight, not screens.
3. **The journal is the hero.** Typography and reading comfort in the editor come
   first; UI chrome recedes. Use the serif to make writing feel like writing.
4. **Delight is earned and rare.** The mascot, wiggles, and micro-interactions are
   seasoning. One small surprise beats ten busy animations. Respect
   `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Sensible accessibility.** No formal WCAG target, but always ship readable
   contrast, visible focus states, real labels, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
